Help & Support

Find answers to common questions and learn how to use Result Viewer easily.

Home Page

Navigate through Result Viewer using the responsive navbar and quick access buttons. The homepage gives you a direct "Check Result Now" shortcut.

View Result

Go to "View Result" and enter your Roll Number and Date of Birth exactly as registered. Your full marksheet will be displayed instantly.

Admin users can log in to add student details, subject marks, grades, and feedback using the Add Result section. Use "Add Subject" to add multiple subjects.

Common Errors

Make sure your Roll Number and Date of Birth match exactly what was registered. Typos or wrong date format may prevent result access.

Forgot Credentials?

Contact your school administration or result management authority if you've forgotten your login details or need a credential reset.

Contact Support

Still facing issues? Reach out to the Result Viewer support team for technical help and assistance. We're happy to help you out.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my result? ▾

Go to the "View Result" page, enter your Roll Number and Date of Birth as registered, then click "View Result". Your marksheet will appear immediately.

What if my result is not found? ▾

Double-check your Roll Number and Date of Birth for typos. If details are correct and result still doesn't show, contact your school's admin team.

Can I print my marksheet? ▾

Yes! Once your result is displayed, click the "Print Result" button. The page will print cleanly without any navigation buttons or UI elements.
